Emergency Rental & Financial Assistance Program

Helping prevent homelessness and support community stability

Overview

We provide urgent financial support to individuals and families facing temporary hardships such as eviction, homelessness, or utility disconnections. Our goal is to stabilize living situations and connect recipients with long-term community resources.

Program Objectives

  • Prevent evictions and reduce homelessness
  • Assist with overdue rent and utility bills
  • Support recovery from job loss or unexpected emergencies
  • Connect to job placement and housing counseling

Eligibility

  • Facing eviction, homelessness, or utility shutoff
  • Loss of income due to job loss, illness, or emergencies
  • Legal lease or utility account holder
  • Household income below 80% AMI

What We Cover

  • Past-due rent and future rent payments
  • Electricity, water, and gas bills
  • Referrals to long-term support

Financial Assistance

Funds will be sent directly to the approved applicant by check or email. Assistance is meant to help you avoid eviction, utility shutoff, and other consequences of financial crises. We aim to get you the help you need quickly and respectfully.
